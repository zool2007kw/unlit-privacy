Unlit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

In short
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.
  • Optional tips are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • You can delete everything at any time from the app.

Who we are

Unlit ("the app") is a free app that helps people stop smoking, vaping or using nicotine pouches. It is published by ZoolVibes ("we", "us"). Contact: zool2007.kw@gmail.com.

Information you enter

To work, the app stores the following on your device only:

WhatWhy
Your quit profile: what you are quitting, how much you used, the price, your currency and quit dateTo calculate your smoke-free time, money saved and milestones
Your reasons for quitting, and optionally a photo you chooseTo show them to you during a craving
Cravings and slips you log (time, trigger, intensity, mood, optional place and notes)To show your history and insights
Savings goals, app settings and notification timesTo show your goals and remind you at the times you choose

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is also excluded from cloud backups. If you choose a photo, the app only gets access to that one photo, through Android's photo picker.

Health information

Some of what you log (for example cravings and slips) relates to your health and habits. Because it stays on your device and is never collected by us, we do not process it. Unlit is a self-help tool and does not provide medical advice.

Optional tips

Every feature of Unlit is free. If you choose to leave a tip, the payment is processed by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your card or payment details. The app only stores, on your device, the fact that you have tipped, so it can say thank you. Google provides us with standard sales reports for tips, which do not include the information you keep in the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): for milestone alerts, reminders at the times you choose and a daily message. You can turn these off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Internet / network state: used only by Google Play's billing library to process optional tips. The app itself does not send your data anywhere.
  • Run at startup / wake lock: used by Android's WorkManager so your scheduled reminders still arrive after your phone restarts.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No accounts, sign-in or location tracking
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export your log as a CSV file from Settings → Export log. The file is saved where you choose; after that, it is yours to manage. You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

External links

The app links to helpful websites such as HSE QUIT, safefood and the NHS. They open in your browser and have their own privacy policies.

Children

Unlit is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct or delete. For tips, you can exercise your rights with Google. If you have any questions, contact us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
